## Verdict: needs a fix cycle

A **new blocking ask** has no earlier F-number and is tied to a quit or a conditional ("mixed") return sentence.

- Dispositions: 1 keep, 9 mixed, 4 quit (one of them TOOL).
- The asks below that rest on no hold are enough for a fix cycle on their own.

### New blocking asks (the features are new since R1)

1. **"Questions for Zehra" removes the Say button and never says so.**
   - Maya stopped on TASTE: "On the desktop with "Questions for Zehra" I got no Say button at all."
   - Tessa stopped CONFUSED: "At two houses on this laptop game I got no way to say anything, and I do not know if that is because I picked Questions for Zehra or something else. The page never says."
   - Samir: "I picked "Questions for Zehra" on the title page and never saw a way to ask her anything."
   - **Proposed fix:**
     - Keep Say in that version, or show a line saying why it is absent.
     - Describe each title-page option under its control.
     - These are 3.x opt-ins by rule. The fix is explanation, not removal.
2. **The "Speak with Zehra" question disappears before the player can answer it.**
   - Leila: "On the desktop it appeared and went within about 15 seconds, and I never answered her."
   - **Proposed fix:** keep the question open until it is answered or dismissed, and slow the clock while it is open.
3. **The phone Send-grain confirmation is squeezed, and the notebook is too short.**
   - Emre: "The Send grain confirmation is squeezed into a narrow column, and the result of sending sits below the visible strip."
   - Samir: "It is about 190 px tall, so Zehra's reply and her "Hold to insist" button are cut off."
   - Malik and Mei said the same.
   - **Proposed fix:** at 360 px wide, show the confirmation full width and give the notebook a minimum height.

### Still open from earlier rounds (F-numbered; for the same fix cycle)

1. **Identical postponement replies, with no way to accept a delay.**
   - Mei: "Let me accept a delay without insisting, so I can tell people to finish their own barley and clear the list of eight identical "Hold to insist" replies."
   - Leila: "Collapse the identical postponement replies into one line".
   - Irene, Pavel and Rosa asked the same.
   - **Proposed fix:**
     - Group identical replies into one row with a count.
     - Add a "Let them finish first" answer that clears the row.
2. **One fixed line to say, which gets "Sit down" or no reply.**
   - Nia: "Make people answer what I say. "Sit down" and the same barley excuse still came back."
   - Aisha: ""I came to listen" gets no reply".
   - Tessa and Diego asked the same.
   - **Proposed fix:** offer two or three sayings per visit, each with a household reply.
3. **A slow clock, and a Skip that is grey or does nothing without saying why.**
   - Diego: "Skip is grey and says nothing about why."
   - Irene: "When Skip does nothing, say so at the button itself".
   - Pavel, Emre, Rosa, Samir and Jonas asked the same.
   - **Proposed fix:** show the reason on the button. Shorten the slowdown when only postponements are open.
4. **Smaller asks:**
   - The ○ and ● circles are unexplained (Pavel, Malik).
   - "another opens when it expires on 24 July" is still reread (Rosa).
   - No enough-before-rain signal (Emre).
   - A call to a starving household cannot be taken back (Aisha).
   - The title-page options are unexplained (most testers).
